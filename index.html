⚡ Everyday Paperwork & Out-of-Court Services

Self-Attestation (Abolition of Notarized Affidavits) ↗ DARPG / Central Gov
📌 What it is

Official central circular replacing notarized court affidavits with simple self-signed declarations for general public services.

🎯 When to use

When government offices, universities, or banks ask for an affidavit for name change, address proof, or basic certificates.

💡 Navigation / Pro Tip

Keep a downloaded copy of this order. Most administrative departments must accept a signed self-declaration—you do not need to pay a court notary.

SHCIL e-Stamping Portal ↗ Online Stamp Paper
📌 What it is

Official digital portal to buy and generate non-judicial stamp papers directly from the Stock Holding Corporation of India.

🎯 When to use

Rent agreements, affidavits, indemnity bonds, power of attorney, and commercial contracts.

💡 Navigation / Pro Tip

Select your state and check if "Home Printing" is available. Pay via UPI/NetBanking to print the certificate directly at home without queuing at court vendor stalls.

NALSA (Free Legal Aid & Lok Adalat) ↗ Dispute Resolution
📌 What it is

Statutory body providing free legal counsel to eligible citizens and organizing out-of-court settlements via Lok Adalats.

🎯 When to use

Settling traffic challans, pending electricity/water bills, minor financial disagreements, and pre-litigation disputes without filing a lawsuit.

💡 Navigation / Pro Tip

Lok Adalat awards have the same status as a civil court decree, incur zero court fees, and are non-appealable (giving final closure).

Tele-Law Service (Dept of Justice) ↗ Pre-Litigation Advice
📌 What it is

Direct video or telephone consultation with certified panel lawyers via village Common Service Centres (CSCs) or mobile app.

🎯 When to use

Before hiring a lawyer, to understand if your situation actually warrants litigation or can be settled administratively.

💡 Navigation / Pro Tip

Free of cost for women, SC/ST, and low-income citizens. You can self-register using an OTP or visit any local CSC center.

📜 Official Bare Acts & Legal Codes

India Code Repository ↗ Master Repository
📌 What it is

The centralized government archive containing every central and state bare act, rule, and amendment in active law.

🎯 When to use

Verifying exact statutory wording for any Indian law (e.g., Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, Consumer Protection, DPDP, IT Act).

💡 Navigation / Pro Tip

Use the search bar and look at the "Status" column to verify the Act is "In Force" and not "Repealed". You can view acts section-by-section or download the full PDF.

eCourts Services Portal ↗ Court Case Tracking
📌 What it is

Official case monitoring portal covering District Courts, High Courts, and judicial tribunals across India.

🎯 When to use

Checking hearing dates, orders, and case progress without traveling to the court registry in person.

💡 Navigation / Pro Tip

Use the 16-character **CNR Number** located at the top of your case acknowledgement slip for one-click access without needing advocate details.
