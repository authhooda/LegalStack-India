🏛️ Citizen Legal & Administrative Guide

Direct official links, clear plain-language guides, and navigation tips to avoid unnecessary courthouse visits.

⚡ Everyday Paperwork & Out-of-Court Services

Self-Attestation Guidelines (Abolition of Notarized Affidavits) ↗ DARPG / Gov Order

What it is: Official Central Government circular mandating self-certification instead of notarized affidavits for routine civil documentation.

When to use: When government departments, colleges, or offices demand a notarized affidavit for name changes, address proof, or basic certificates.

💡 Pro Tip: Keep a PDF copy or printout of this official guideline. Most standard administrative forms accept a self-signed declaration without visiting a court notary.
SHCIL e-Stamping System ↗ Online Stamp Paper

What it is: Official portal by Stock Holding Corporation of India to purchase and print non-judicial stamp papers online.

When to use: Rental agreements, power of attorney, basic business contracts, or declarations.

💡 Pro Tip: Check whether your state supports "Home Printing". If yes, you can pay online via UPI/NetBanking and print your e-stamp directly on legal paper without queuing at stamp vendors.
NALSA (Free Legal Aid & Lok Adalat) ↗ Alternative Dispute Resolution

What it is: National Legal Services Authority providing free legal representation and pre-litigation settlement through Lok Adalats.

When to use: Settling minor disputes (traffic challans, electricity/water bill disputes, insurance claims, petty financial disagreements) without filing court cases.

💡 Pro Tip: Settlements reached in Lok Adalat are final and binding, have no court fees, and cannot be appealed—saving years of litigation.
Tele-Law Service (Dept of Justice) ↗ Direct Legal Advice

What it is: Connects citizens directly with panel lawyers for pre-litigation advice via video call or telephone at village Common Service Centres (CSCs).

When to use: Getting early legal advice to understand if your matter actually requires filing a case before hiring an expensive private lawyer.

💡 Pro Tip: Free for women, SC/ST, and low-income individuals. You can register directly using your mobile number or visit your nearest CSC kiosk.

📜 Official Bare Acts & Legal Codes

India Code Repository ↗ Master Repository

What it is: The single official digital archive for all Central and State Bare Acts, sub-rules, and notifications.

When to use: Finding the exact, current text of any Indian law (e.g., BNS, Contract Act, DPDP Act, Companies Act).

💡 Pro Tip: Click on "Acts by Year" or use the search bar to type the Act name. Look for the "Repealed" or "In Force" tag next to the Act title to avoid referencing outdated laws.
The Constitution of India (Official Text) ↗ Fundamental Law

What it is: Full bilingual (English/Hindi) text of the Constitution with all latest Constitutional Amendments.

When to use: Researching Fundamental Rights (Articles 12–35), Directive Principles, or structure of governance.

💡 Pro Tip: Always verify that the PDF downloaded has the updated year on the title page to ensure it includes the latest constitutional amendments.
eCourts Services Portal ↗ Case Tracking

What it is: National database to track case statuses, orders, and daily cause lists for High Courts and District Courts.

When to use: Tracking a live court case without visiting the registry physically.

💡 Pro Tip: Keep your 16-digit **CNR Number** handy (found on your case receipt). Entering the CNR number gives instant access to case history and next hearing dates.
